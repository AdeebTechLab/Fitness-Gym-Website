I'm new to working out. Can I still join?

Absolutely. Our coaches can help you find a comfortable starting point, show you how the equipment works, and adapt movements to your experience and pace.

What should I bring for my first workout?

Wear clothes you can move in and bring a water bottle. We can show you around when you arrive, so there is no need to have a routine figured out in advance.

Do I need to book a class ahead of time?

Booking ahead is a good idea for coached small-group classes because places are limited. Open gym access is available according to your membership.

Can I try the studio before choosing a plan?

Yes. Use the “Book a tour” link to find the visit details and explore the studio before choosing a membership.

Can I change my membership later?

Membership options are designed to fit different routines. Get in touch with the team to discuss available changes and the terms for your plan.
